# INV-003 · Relationships — provisional implementation profile

Registered predicates: `contains`, `depends-on`, `implements`, `represents`, `governed-by`. Endpoints are immutable meron UUIDs. Duplicates and self edges are forbidden. A retired endpoint cannot acquire new edges. Removes retain an audit event rather than an active edge.

`contains` originates at a system, gives its target at most one parent, and is acyclic. NOI cannot be contained; Invariants and Governance can only be contained by NOI. `depends-on` is separately acyclic. `implements` targets an invariant. `represents` connects a document to an invariant. `governed-by` targets the Governance system identity and has no authority effect. Structure is explicit; namespace segments do not require synthetic merons.

Rust checks endpoint shape and asks Governance about both endpoints. PostgreSQL checks references, cardinality and recursive cycles under serialized mutations. Invariant conformance and governance interpretation are outside relationship validation. Retirement rejects incoming edges and outgoing containment until explicitly resolved; this prevents dangling structural references.
